1. Grant Rate — An Honest View

Honest note: We do not promise "guaranteed grant." Grant decisions are made by examiners under statutory standards. Firms that guarantee grants usually do so by drastically narrowing the scope of protection — a granted patent that doesn't protect your technology is worthless. Our approach: free prior-art search and patentability assessment first, and we will tell you honestly your chances and whether the application is worth filing, before you decide to instruct us.
