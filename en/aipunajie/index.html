Who We Are

Beijing Aipunajie Patent Agency (Special General Partnership) was founded in 2012, headquartered in Dongcheng District, Beijing. Building on the Najie brand, it is a dual-qualified professional service firm holding both a formal patent agency qualification and a foreign-related patent agency qualification.

We focus on providing innovative enterprises, universities and research institutes with one-stop patent, trademark and copyright services — rights establishment, enforcement and risk control. We insist on quality, high standards and reliable delivery to help innovations convert into value.
