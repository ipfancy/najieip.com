August 12, 2026 · IP Law · Aipunajie Patent Firm / Mili Law Firm

We are pleased to announce: Meili Law Firm Changping Branch has recently been officially approved for establishment.

Why Changping

The Changping District is a core node of Beijing's northern science and technology innovation corridor, home to three national-level innovation platforms: Future Science City, Zhongguancun Changping Park, and Life Science Park. This area not only hosts a large number of high-tech enterprises but also boasts dense clusters of research institutes and university resources.

For Meili, the strategic significance of the Changping Branch lies in three aspects:

I. Proximity to the Source of Innovation. Changping is home to thousands of technology-driven enterprises, spanning from biomedicine to advanced manufacturing, from artificial intelligence to new energy—these are precisely the sectors with the strongest demand for intellectual property legal services. The branch office is right by our clients' side.

II. Radiating Across the Northern Sci-Tech Innovation Belt. The Changping Branch forms a north-south complement to the Haidian headquarters, covering Beijing's northern technology corridor. From Xi'erqi to Future Science City, from Huilongguan to Shahe Higher Education Park, Meili's service radius has been further expanded.

III. Extending Comprehensive Legal Services Downward. As a full-service law firm (not limited to IP), the Changping Branch will provide legal services across all fields, including administrative law, criminal law, maritime law, antitrust, anti-unfair competition, international law, and the Civil Code, serving enterprises and residents in Changping and surrounding areas.
