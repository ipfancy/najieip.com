Who We Are

Beijing Najie Intellectual Property Agency Co., Ltd. is a professional legal institution providing IP services, founded in 2010. Our team comprises experienced and skilled patent agents, trademark agents and attorneys.

Our core team includes senior agents who worked at renowned foreign-related IP firms and former CNIPA examiners, including the first batch of patent litigation attorneys recommended and registered with the Supreme People's Court of the PRC. They studied IP law in the US, Japan, Europe and Hong Kong, master multiple languages, and have experience serving multinational corporations.

Our technical fields cover machinery, electronics, telecommunications, computers, networks, semiconductors, optics, electrical, materials, chemistry, biotechnology, pharmaceuticals, metallurgy, chemicals and new energy.
